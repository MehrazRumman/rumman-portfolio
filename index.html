About

I'm a mathematics graduate turned backend engineer. At Adfinix I've built two ad-tech products from the ground up, Adsloft and Aifinix, covering everything from OpenRTB bidding flows and event ingestion to the analytics dashboards that advertisers and publishers actually read.

Outside of work I contribute to the Python ecosystem I depend on. Through Google Summer of Code I shipped HTTP caching to pyOBIS for the Integrated Ocean Observing System, then took the work on stage as a keynote speaker at Hacking Limnology 2025. I've also landed patches in Django, Django REST Framework, DRF-extensions, Scrapy and Celery.

I like problems where performance, correctness and data meet, and I've been teaching Python to students since 2022 because explaining something is the best way to understand it.
